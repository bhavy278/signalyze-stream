# /brag plan — Signalyze Stream

**Mode:** brag-slim (self-contained render; no Hyperframes)
**Tone:** polished — serious, elegant, restrained
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, ~20s
**Music:** happy-beats-business-moves vol.1 (120 BPM, royalty-free, bundled)

## What it is
Signalyze Stream — an event-driven, AI document-intelligence platform. Upload a
contract and get a structured breakdown (parties, key terms, risk-flagged
clauses), read it in an in-app viewer, and ask questions with answers grounded
in cited passages. Java 21 Spring Boot microservices over Kafka, MongoDB, Redis,
OpenAI, Next.js.

## Angle
Treat it as a real product launch. Lead with the outcome (understand any
document in seconds), then show the actual product doing its job — the
structured analysis and the grounded Q&A — and close on the engineering.

## Hook (first 2s)
Warm canvas, coral dot, the Signalyze wordmark rises in with the line
"Understand any document in seconds." Calm, confident, product-grade.
